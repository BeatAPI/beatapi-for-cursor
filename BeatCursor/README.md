# BeatCursor

Use your [BeatAPI](https://beatapi.io) account inside Cursor.

- **Sign in once** — no key pasting. Authorization happens in your browser.
- **Every text model**, with its context window and price, straight from your account.
- **Balance and usage** in the sidebar; top up with `⌘⌥B`.

Open the BeatAPI panel from the activity bar to get started.
